EdgeClose Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 23, 2026

What EdgeClose does

EdgeClose closes website tabs that you configure after the selected period of inactivity. It processes browser tab URLs only to determine whether a tab matches one of your configured website patterns.

Data stored

EdgeClose stores your website patterns, timeout settings, warning settings, and sound preferences in Microsoft Edge extension storage on your device. It also stores temporary activity timestamps for monitored tabs. This data is not sold or used for advertising.

Data shared

EdgeClose does not send browsing history, page contents, keystrokes, mouse actions, or stored settings to the developer or any analytics provider. The Options page may request public release metadata from the GitHub API to display available EdgeClose versions; no user data is included in that request.

Your controls

You control which websites are monitored, the timeout, the warning period, and warning sound. Removing configured websites stops future monitoring. Removing the extension removes its stored extension data according to Edge's extension storage behavior.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact published with the EdgeClose listing.
